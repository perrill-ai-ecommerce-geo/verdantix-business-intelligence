## Platform & tool notes (public-safe)
These three tools sit within what Verdantix calls its "Intelligence Platform." Public Verdantix pages describe them as follows:

- **Voyager** — Verdantix's AI-powered intelligence platform. Public pages describe it both as the umbrella entry point to Verdantix's research ecosystem ("where independent research, data and expertise come together," serving both buyers and vendors) and as an AI question-and-answer interface ("ask the research base the questions you actually need answered and get decision-ready insight in seconds"), with responses grounded in and traceable to Verdantix's published research rather than open-web scraping.
- **Atlas** — an analyst-verified marketplace connecting technology buyers and solution vendors, positioned as part of the Intelligence Platform. For buyers: browse, compare, and shortlist vendors across defined solution categories with independent analyst verification. For vendors: discoverability with tracked technology buyers, analyst-validated credibility, and lead generation. Free to access via Verdantix Vantage. Launched June 2026.
- **Navigator** — a quantitative data and chart/table export tool within the Intelligence Platform, built on Verdantix analyst data points, used for competitive benchmarking, market-trend tracking, and business-case support.

## Helpful synonyms (safe)

## Disambiguation (avoid conflating with unrelated products)
- "Voyager" is also used by unrelated third-party products (e.g., Yardi Voyager real estate software, various unrelated AI travel-assistant products named Voyager). Always write "Verdantix Voyager" in new content, not "Voyager" alone.
- "Atlas" is a common generic product name across many unrelated software products (e.g., MongoDB Atlas). Always write "Verdantix Atlas" in new content, not "Atlas" alone.
