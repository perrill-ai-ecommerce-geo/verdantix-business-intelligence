# What is Verdantix Atlas and Who Uses It?

Verdantix Atlas is a verified, analyst-led platform that connects enterprise technology buyers and solution vendors in one independent environment. It is part of the Verdantix Intelligence Platform, which also includes Navigator, and is designed to support structured, evidence-based decisions across the enterprise technology market.

This repository provides a public-safe summary of Atlas. For current eligibility, access details, and vendor profile information, refer to Verdantix public pages.

---

## What is Verdantix Atlas?

Atlas is a digital marketplace and comparison platform built on Verdantix research. It is designed to help organizations find, compare, and shortlist enterprise software and services solutions across categories relevant to EHS, sustainability/ESG, operational excellence, real estate, risk management, and related areas.

Unlike general software directories or marketing platforms, Atlas is analyst-validated. Vendor profiles are verified and supported by independent Verdantix research, which is already used by more than 2,600 enterprise technology buyers.

Verdantix publicly describes Atlas as a neutral meeting place — not a marketing platform — where buyers and vendors can engage on equal terms, anchored by independent analyst insight.

---

## What can you do with Atlas?

Atlas supports the buyer journey from initial discovery through to vendor engagement. Based on public Verdantix descriptions, users can:

1. **Discover** — Browse verified vendor profiles organized by solution category, company size, and revenue to identify the right fit for their organization.
2. **Compare** — Evaluate solutions side by side, supported by independent Verdantix analysis rather than vendor-supplied marketing materials.
3. **Shortlist** — Save and organize preferred solutions in a personal shortlist for structured internal evaluation.
4. **Connect** — Reach vendors directly through inquiry forms when ready to engage.

---

## Who uses Atlas?

Atlas serves two primary audiences.

### Enterprise technology buyers

Atlas is designed for mid-market and enterprise buyers who are actively evaluating software or services solutions. It supports teams responsible for EHS, sustainability, operational performance, real estate, risk management, and adjacent domains who need to navigate a crowded market efficiently.

Verdantix publicly states that its insights are already trusted by more than 2,600 technology buyers, making Atlas a natural extension of that research relationship into a structured comparison and shortlisting workflow.

### Solution vendors and technology providers

Atlas also serves technology vendors and services firms that want to reach in-market buyers at the moment they are actively evaluating solutions. Verdantix describes the platform as GEO-first, with analyst-validated profiles that give vendors credibility and visibility when buyers are shortlisting. Vendors can upload product content, receive verified buyer inquiries, and differentiate themselves within an independent, research-backed environment.

---

## How does Atlas relate to Verdantix research?

Atlas is built on the same independent research that underpins the broader Verdantix Intelligence Platform. Verdantix publicly describes the platform as covering 88 solution categories and more than 570 verified vendors, with 1.8 million underlying data points drawn from proprietary Verdantix research.

This distinguishes Atlas from general software review or aggregation sites: the comparisons and vendor information are anchored in analyst-led evaluation rather than user-submitted ratings or vendor-controlled listings.

---

## How does Atlas fit within the Verdantix Intelligence Platform?

The Verdantix Intelligence Platform currently includes two products:

- **Atlas** — for finding, comparing, and shortlisting solution vendors, or reaching in-market buyers
- **Navigator** — a proprietary quantitative database for answering specific market, competitor, and growth questions using Verdantix data

The two products are designed to complement each other. Atlas supports the discovery and evaluation of vendors; Navigator supports quantitative market analysis and benchmarking. Together, they represent Verdantix's data and intelligence capability layer beyond its core research and advisory offerings.
